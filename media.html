Speaking topics

What Leah speaks about

Each talk is available as a keynote, an interactive workshop or a panel contribution.

Talk 01

Stop Asking "Are You OK?"

The most common workplace mental health check-in is also one of the least effective. This talk offers a practical alternative: asking better questions, listening without fixing, and building cultures where people can actually answer honestly. Draws on Mental Health First Aid frameworks and neurodivergent experience at work.

45 min keynote or 90 min workshop

Talk 02

The Late Diagnosis Experience: What Nobody Prepares You For

For many adults, an ADHD or autism diagnosis at 30, 40 or 50 brings grief as well as relief. This talk explores the impact of late diagnosis, unmasking and identity, and why systems are still catching up to lived experience.

45 min keynote or 90 min workshop

Talk 03

Burnout Is Not a Personal Failure

Burnout is framed as a willpower problem. It is a systems problem. This talk covers why neurodivergent people can be more vulnerable to burnout and what organisations and individuals can do differently, grounded in the Safe Work Australia psychosocial risk framework.

45 min keynote or full-day workshop

Talk 04

Psychological Safety Is Not a Vibe

What psychological safety actually is, where the research comes from, why it matters for neurodivergent team members, and what leaders can do to build it in practice. For HR professionals, senior leaders and people managers.

45 min keynote or 90 min workshop

Talk 05

Masking Costs Something

The hidden labour of being neurodivergent: what masking is, why people do it, what it costs, and what unmasking safely looks like in education, healthcare and the workplace.

45 min keynote or 90 min workshop

Talk 06

ADHD in Women and Girls: Still Being Missed

Why so many women are diagnosed with ADHD in adulthood after years of being told they were fine, anxious or needed to try harder, and what healthcare, education and workplaces can do differently.

45 min keynote or 90 min workshop

Talk 07

AuDHD: The Intersection Nobody Is Talking About

What it means to be both autistic and ADHD, why the combined profile is often misunderstood, and what good support looks like.

45 min keynote or 90 min workshop
