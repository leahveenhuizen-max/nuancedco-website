What this is

Real conversation. Real support.

Nuanced Co. consultancy sessions are not therapy. They are not coaching in the traditional sense. They are structured, focused conversations with someone who brings both clinical knowledge and lived AuDHD experience to the table.

Leah is a Registered Nurse who was diagnosed AuDHD as an adult. She understands the gap between what the clinical literature says and what actually happens in your life. Sessions are built around that gap.

This is a space where you do not have to explain yourself from scratch. Where the starting assumption is that your brain is not broken, and the goal is to figure out what actually works for it.

Who this is for

You might be in the right place if...

  • You have an ADHD or AuDHD diagnosis and you are still figuring out what it actually means for your life
  • You were diagnosed as an adult and you are processing years of not understanding why things felt so hard
  • You keep burning out and you want to understand why, not just survive the next one
  • You have tried therapy and coaching and left feeling like the professional did not really understand how your brain works
  • You want someone to be honest with you, not just tell you what you want to hear
  • You are tired of generic strategies that work for neurotypical people and make things worse for you
What this is not

Important to know

Consultancy is not therapy and does not replace clinical mental health support. Leah is a Registered Nurse, not a psychologist or psychiatrist. Sessions are educational, supportive, and practical in nature.

If you are in crisis or need clinical mental health intervention, your GP is the right starting point. Leah can help you navigate that system and advocate for yourself within it, but she is not a substitute for it.

Pricing is available on request. Leah is committed to access and will discuss options with anyone for whom cost is a barrier.
