Nuanced Co.

Common questions

Answers to common questions about Nuanced Co., educational resources, courses, community updates and working with Leah.

Nuanced Co. is an Australian neurodivergent consultancy founded by Leah Veenhuizen. We create evidence-informed educational resources, self-paced courses and individual consultancy for adults navigating late ADHD diagnosis, masking, grief and support needs. Our work is educational and reflective. It is not therapy or clinical care.

No. Nuanced Co. provides educational and reflective support only. Our courses, guides and consultancy sessions are not therapy, counselling, diagnosis, clinical treatment or crisis support. If you are experiencing significant distress, please seek support from a qualified health professional or contact a crisis service.

Nuanced Co. works with neurodivergent adults, particularly those who have received an ADHD, autism or AuDHD diagnosis later in life and are still making sense of what that means. You do not need a formal diagnosis to access our resources. Self-identified neurodivergent experiences are welcome.

Nuanced Co. is based in Geelong, Victoria, Australia. Leah works with people across Australia and internationally. All digital courses and resources are available wherever you are.

The Unmasked Life is a four-week self-paced educational course for late-diagnosed ADHD adults. It covers recognition, grief, masking, shame and building practical support around your actual brain. The course is currently in preparation. Join the waitlist to be notified when enrolment opens.

Yes. Leah offers individual consultancy sessions for neurodivergent adults within an educational and reflective scope. Sessions are not therapy or clinical care. You can book a call to find out if it is a good fit before committing.

Nuanced Co. publishes digital guides for the early "I need language for this" stage of late diagnosis. The Late Diagnosis Life Review is available now through our Stan store. New guides are added over time. See all free resources or browse the Stan store for paid guides.

Join the Nuanced Co. community updates list to hear about future group programs, peer spaces and new resources. For The Unmasked Life course specifically, join the course waitlist.

Nuanced Co. provides educational and reflective content only. Nothing on this site constitutes clinical advice, diagnosis, therapy, counselling, treatment, medication advice or crisis support. If you are in distress, please contact a qualified health professional or a crisis service.
