Work with me

Support that starts with understanding how your brain works.

No scripts. No generic checklists. Support built around what is actually happening for you, with someone who genuinely gets it.

For individuals

Real conversation. Real support.

Nuanced Co. consultation sessions are structured, focused conversations with someone who brings both clinical knowledge and lived AuDHD experience to the table. Leah is a Registered Nurse who was diagnosed AuDHD as an adult. She understands the gap between what the clinical literature says and what actually happens in your life.

This is a space where you do not have to explain yourself from scratch. The starting assumption is that your brain is not broken, and the goal is to figure out what actually works for it.

The process

How it works

Discovery call (free, 20 minutes)

A no-pressure conversation to understand where you are and whether consultation is the right fit. No commitment required.

Your first session

We go deep on what is actually happening for you. No intake forms the length of a tax return. Just conversation, and listening.

A real plan

You leave each session with something concrete. Not a list of things to try harder at, but a framework built around your brain.

Ongoing support (your choice)

Some people want a one-off session for clarity. Others want ongoing support. There is no pressure either way.

Who this is for

You might be in the right place if...

  • You have an ADHD, autism or AuDHD diagnosis, or you're self-identified, and you're still figuring out what it means for your life
  • You were diagnosed as an adult and you're processing years of not understanding why things felt so hard
  • You keep burning out and you want to understand why, not just survive the next one
  • You've left other support feeling like the professional didn't really understand how your brain works
  • You want someone to be honest with you, not just tell you what you want to hear
  • You're tired of generic strategies that work for neurotypical people and make things worse for you

For workplaces

Move beyond awareness. Build genuine psychological safety.

Practical, evidence-based consulting for Australian organisations that want neurodivergent inclusion to mean more than a poster in the break room. It is grounded in the WHS Act 2011, the Disability Discrimination Act 1992 and current Australian psychosocial risk regulations, and in what those regulations look like for a neurodivergent person sitting in a meeting that wasn't designed for their brain.

Assessment

Psychosocial risk review

A structured review of your workplace practices against psychosocial risk obligations, with practical recommendations.

Training

Neurodivergent inclusion training

Evidence-based training for leaders and HR teams on ADHD, autism and AuDHD at work, moving beyond awareness into practice.

Policy

Reasonable adjustments framework

Building or reviewing your adjustments process so it actually works, including scripts, templates and guidance for managers.

Consulting

Individual workplace support

Support for neurodivergent employees navigating disclosure, adjustment requests, performance plans and workplace conflict.
