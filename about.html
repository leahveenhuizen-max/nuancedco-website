Meet your founder

Hey! I'm Leah…

I'm a Registered Nurse, AuDHD person, and the founder of Nuanced Co., an adult neurodivergent consultancy based in Geelong, Australia. I received my own late diagnosis after years of working in clinical environments and still not being able to explain why the systems around me never quite fit.

That experience became the foundation of The Unmasked Life™. Not a workaround, not a productivity hack, and definitely not another self-help system built for a neurotypical nervous system. A structural methodology: evidence-based, warm, and designed around how your brain actually works.

I built this course because I couldn't find what I needed when I was diagnosed. So I built it for both of us.

About Leah

Hi, I'm Leah.

I'm a Registered Nurse, an AuDHD person, and the founder of Nuanced Co.

I spent years working in healthcare watching people get half-answers and full blame for the way their brains work. I'm doing something different here.

Nuanced Co. exists because neurodivergent people deserve support that actually understands them. Not support designed for someone else and quietly adapted.

I'm based in Geelong and work with people across Australia and internationally.
