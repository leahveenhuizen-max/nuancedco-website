Meet your founder

Hi, I'm Leah.

Neurodivergent consultant · Registered Nurse · Mental health educator

I'm a Registered Nurse, AuDHD person, and the founder of Nuanced Co., an adult neurodivergent consultancy based in Geelong, Australia. I received my own late diagnosis after years of working in clinical environments and still not being able to explain why the systems around me never quite fit.

That experience became the foundation of my work at Nuanced Co. Not a workaround, not a productivity hack, and definitely not another self-help system built for a neurotypical nervous system. A structural approach: evidence-based, warm, and designed around how your brain actually works.

I built this work because I couldn't find what I needed when I was diagnosed. So I built it for both of us.

I'm based in Geelong and work with people across Australia and internationally.
