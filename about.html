About Leah

I'm a Registered Nurse.
An AuDHD person.
And someone who gets it.

I'm a Registered Nurse, an AuDHD person, and the founder of Nuanced Co. I built this because I kept seeing people like me get handed the wrong map.

"I spent years watching people get half-answers and full blame for the way their brains work."

My story

I spent years watching the system fail people like me.

As a Registered Nurse, I sat across from patients who were clearly AuDHD: smart, perceptive, and exhausted. I watched them leave with a pamphlet and a referral and no real understanding of what was actually going on for them.

Then I started connecting those experiences to my own. The hyperfocus. The sensory overwhelm. The way I could memorise an entire ward's worth of patient history but forget to eat lunch. The burnout that snuck up on me and then hit like a freight train.

I am AuDHD. And once I understood that (really understood it, not just as a diagnosis but as an identity) everything shifted.

Nuanced Co. exists because that shift should be available to everyone, not just the people lucky enough to stumble into the right information at the right time.

What I believe

The values behind Nuanced Co.

01
Identity over deficit

Being neurodivergent is not a problem to be fixed. It is a way of being in the world with real strengths, real challenges, and real support needs that are too often misunderstood.

02
Practical over performative

Awareness is not support. Understanding without action changes nothing. Every interaction with Nuanced Co. is built to give you something you can actually use.

03
Access without conditions

Good information and genuine support should not require a diagnosis, a referral, or a specific level of functioning. You deserve to be met where you are.
