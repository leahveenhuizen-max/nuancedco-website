Your brain has a name

Who I work with

Support tailored to your specific neurodivergent profile. Every brain is wired differently — your diagnosis isn't just a label, it's the foundation for understanding how you actually work.

ADHD

Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder

The neurodivergence of dopamine regulation. Executive function differences, attention management, and the gap between intention and follow-through.

Executive Function Attention Time Blindness Hyperfocus Impulse

AuDHD

Autism + ADHD Co-occurrence

The intersection of two distinct neurodivergences. Combining social/sensory differences with executive function challenges — a unique profile that deserves specific understanding.

Dual Wiring Sensory + Attention Social Masking Regulation Complexity High Support Needs

Autism

Autism Spectrum Condition

Differences in social processing, sensory experience, and pattern recognition. A different way of thinking that often goes unrecognized in late-diagnosed adults.

Sensory Processing Social Communication Pattern Recognition Deep Focus Literal Thinking
