4-week self-paced course

The Unmasked Life™

A course for late-diagnosed ADHD adults

Four weeks of evidence-informed education and structured reflection. Understand your brain. Grieve what went unsupported. Unlearn the shame. Build a life that actually fits.

Who this course is for

You have a diagnosis.
Now what?

You received your ADHD diagnosis as an adult. Maybe recently. Maybe a while ago. Either way, you are still trying to make sense of what it means for your actual life,the past, the present, and how things might work differently from here.

This course is for you if:

  • You have received an ADHD diagnosis as an adult and are still processing what it means
  • You are looking back at your life through a new lens and feeling a mix of relief, grief, anger or confusion
  • You have noticed patterns of masking, overcompensating or performing fine for a long time
  • You want practical, grounded education,not a symptom checklist or a productivity hack
  • You are waiting for assessment and want useful education in the meantime

Course structure

Four weeks. Four themes.

Each week follows a four-stage framework: Recognise, Acknowledge, Understand, Move Forward. You work at your own pace. There are no deadlines and no time limits.

Week 1

It Finally Makes Sense

"What if I was never broken, just unsupported?"

Milestone: Map three ADHD patterns in your life that you previously misunderstood.

Week 2

Grief for What Could Have Been

"What am I allowed to grieve?"

Milestone: Name one area of grief and one form of support you wish you had received.

Week 3

The Mask, the Shame and the Big Feelings

"What have I been carrying, hiding or performing?"

Milestone: Identify one mask, one shame story and one emotional pattern you are ready to notice differently.

Week 4

Building a Life That Fits

"What support does my actual brain need now?"

Milestone: Build one ADHD-friendly support system for a real part of your life.

Your consultant

Leah Veenhuizen

Registered Nurse,Neurodivergent Consultant,Nuanced Co.

Leah is a registered nurse, mental health educator and neurodivergent consultant, currently completing postgraduate counselling studies. She is the founder of Nuanced Co., a neurodivergent consultancy creating Australian-first, evidence-informed education and reflective resources for late-diagnosed ADHD adults. She is also AuDHD herself.

Educational and reflective only. Not diagnosis, therapy, counselling, treatment, medication advice, clinical advice or crisis support. This course does not replace care from a qualified health professional. If you need support now, visit the support page.
