28-Day Journal WorkbookThe Unmasked Life™ , A Foundational Course for Newly Diagnosed Adults

Unpacking and the Grief. Write as much or as little as you need each day. Your entries save automatically.

Day 01

What parts of your public persona require the most physical or vocal energy to maintain?

Day 02

Identify one sound, lighting setup, or texture today that caused subtle physical tension. What is the immediate antidote?

Day 03

Where did you try to use sheer willpower today to override mental friction? How can we restructure that task tomorrow using a low-bar entry?

Day 04

Allow yourself to look back at a past struggle with your new integration vocabulary. What changes when you view that moment through neurology instead of failure?

Day 05

Which task today felt effortlessly engaging? Was it driven by Interest, Novelty, Challenge, Urgency, or Passion?

Day 06

Notice the exact hour your energy cratered today. Did you try to force linear output, or did you lean into rhythmic adaptation?

Day 07

Describe how your body feels when you are completely alone in a sensory-optimized room. Let that feeling become your baseline.

The Brain and the Cost of Masking. Days 8 to 14.

Day 08

What is the very first physical tell your body gives when entering a yellow state? (e.g., tight jaw, shallow breathing, cold hands?)

Day 09

Where did you try to force compliance today when your body was clearly in an Autonomic Defensive State? What happened?

Day 10

Take a strict 10-minute Zero-Input Intermission today. Describe how your mental clarity shifts when sensory demands are entirely removed.

Day 11

When you hit heavy brain fog today, did you need sensory reduction (State Red triage) or a high-contrast novelty injection (State Yellow triage)?

Day 12

Which interaction today required the heaviest internal editing or masking? How long did it take your nervous system to settle afterward?

Day 13

Identify one small expectation or demand you chose not to participate in today to protect your battery baseline.

Day 14

Reflect on your environmental surroundings right now. Name three specific physical items or settings that make your body feel instantly settled and secure.

Identity and the Support Map. Days 15 to 21.

Day 15

Describe the exact task that triggered a state of paralysis today. What was your brain trying to protect you from?

Day 16

Did an upcoming event today cause you to lose your morning to Waiting Mode? How did your body feel during that waiting state?

Day 17

Choose one intimidating task tomorrow. What is a micro-step for it that is so small it feels entirely silly to write down?

Day 18

Which specific transition today felt the most physically jarring? (e.g., leaving the house, stopping a hyperfocus state, starting work?)

Day 19

Try the Zero-Commitment Rule today. Did you choose to stop after the micro-step, or did your brain naturally spark momentum?

Day 20

When did you misjudge time today? Did you feel rushed and panicked, or did you over-prepare and freeze early?

Day 21

Reflect on an action today that required absolutely zero willpower to start. What made that transition so fluid?

Integration and Sustainable Accommodation. Days 22 to 28.

Day 22

What is one physical tool or environmental modification that instantly lowers your daily executive load?

Day 23

Where did you feel a wave of guilt for making things easier for yourself today? Rewrite that choice using your new integration vocabulary.

Day 24

Who is the one person in your life with whom you feel entirely safe dropping your verbal and physical mask completely?

Day 25

When your system enters a state of deep, red-zone burnout, what are the absolute minimum survival baselines you need to secure?

Day 26

Reflect on a boundary you set this week. Did it protect your energetic battery, or did you compromise to avoid social friction?

Day 27

Look at your upcoming month. What is one high-demand transition you can prepare a low-sensory runway for in advance?

Day 28

Take a deep breath and scan your body. Describe the difference between the heavy mask you wore on Day 1 and the grounded skin you are standing in today.
