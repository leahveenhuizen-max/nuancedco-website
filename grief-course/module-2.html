Recognise the losses

ExerciseMove forward: your losses map

Make a "losses map" with five headings: work, study, relationships, family and self. Write one or two losses under each, including anything masking cost you. You don't need to fill every box. Choose one loss you'd feel comfortable naming in this week's live session, if you want to.

ReflectionReflection prompts

Your notes are private and saved in this browser on this device only.

  1. Which loss surprised you the most when you wrote it down?

  2. What has masking cost you that no one else could see?

  3. What would you say to a friend who had the same losses map?
