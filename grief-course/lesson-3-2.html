Envy of people diagnosed early, who got support and language you didn't, is common and rarely talked about. It isn't petty. Envy often points to something you needed and didn't get.

The "what if" loop sounds like: what if someone had noticed at eight, fifteen or twenty-five? What if I'd finished that degree, kept that job, stayed in that relationship?

The loop is your brain trying to make sense of the loss. A few ways to work with it:

  • Notice it and name it: "There's the what-if loop again"
  • Let it finish one thought, then gently bring yourself back to today
  • Ask "What does this what-if tell me I need now?"
