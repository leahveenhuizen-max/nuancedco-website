In one study, autistic adults described feeling happier and more at ease with autistic friends and family (Crompton et al., 2020 (opens in a new tab)). In an Australian study, newly diagnosed autistic adults found shared stories from other autistic people helped them feel they belonged (Edwards et al., 2025 (opens in a new tab)). Finding your people isn't a bonus. It's part of healing.

If you're autistic: connection may feel best in small, predictable, low-demand spaces.

If you're ADHD: it may thrive on shared activities, novelty or body doubling.

If you're both: look for spaces that offer some of each.

Ideas to start: an online neurodivergent community, a local meetup, a hobby group, or the friendships you've already started in this course's live sessions.
