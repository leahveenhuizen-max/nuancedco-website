A note from Leah: [Leah to add: what helped, what didn't, and where she is now.]

You might feel settled for weeks, then a memory, a date or a conversation brings it all back. That's not going backwards. Healthy grieving swings between facing the loss and getting on with life (Stroebe & Schut, 1999).

Many people describe still ebbing and flowing between shame, grief and anger long after diagnosis, while noticing their baseline slowly shifting towards more self-compassion and less confusion. That's what moving forward often looks like: not a finish line, but a gentler baseline.

The younger you was doing their best without a map. Self-compassion isn't letting yourself off the hook. It's treating yourself the way you'd treat a friend who had been through the same thing.
