ExerciseMove forward: an unsent letter

Write an unsent letter to a system, a person, or your younger self. Say what you need to say, then choose what to do with it: keep it, tear it up, or read part of it out in the live session. Afterwards, move your body for five minutes.

ReflectionReflection prompts

Your notes are private and saved in this browser on this device only.

  1. Who or what are you most angry at right now?

  2. What does your envy tell you about what you needed?

  3. Which "what if" keeps coming back, and what would you say to it?
