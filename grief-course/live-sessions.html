How each session runs

One 60-minute session per module, run the same way each week so it feels predictable and safe.

  1. Arrive (5 min): welcome, the group agreement, and a reminder of the crisis resources
  2. Check in (10 min): one word for how you're arriving, typed or spoken
  3. Leah shares (10 min): a short reflection on this week's module
  4. Group discussion (25 min): the module's reflection prompts, with cameras and mics optional
  5. One small step (5 min): everyone names their Move forward step
  6. Close (5 min): a grounding moment and where to find support between sessions

Group agreement:

  • What's shared in the group stays in the group
  • Speak from your own experience, no advice-giving or diagnosing each other
  • Cameras, mics and sharing are always optional
  • This is peer support and education, not therapy

You are always welcome to take part by chat only.

Scope note: This course offers education and peer connection from lived experience. It is not therapy, counselling, diagnosis or medical advice. If you need more support, please speak with your GP or one of the services below.
