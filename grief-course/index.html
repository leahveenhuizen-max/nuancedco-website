Grief After Diagnosis: A Course for AuDHD Adults

Written lessons, short videos from Leah, and one live group session a week.

A five-module course for autistic, ADHD and AuDHD adults who have been diagnosed later in life and feel lost about what comes next. It is built from Leah's lived experience, with research woven through, and runs as written lessons plus one live group session a week.

  • Who it's for: autistic, ADHD and AuDHD adults in the months or years after a late diagnosis. It's written through an AuDHD lens, with notes wherever experiences tend to differ. It's especially for anyone feeling relief and grief at the same time and unsure where to turn.
  • What it is: education, reflection and peer connection, guided by a Registered Nurse and Master of Counselling student who has been through it.
  • What it isn't: therapy, counselling, diagnosis or medical advice. Anyone who needs more support is pointed to their GP and the services in the resources section.

What you get

  • Five modules, one released each week
  • 16 short lessons in total (3 to 4 per module, about 5 to 10 minutes each)
  • A short video from Leah in every lesson, with the written text underneath for people who prefer reading
  • One exercise and reflection prompts per module
  • One 60-minute live group session each week

About Leah

Leah Veenhuizen is a Registered Nurse, Mental Health First Aid facilitator and Master of Counselling student. She is AuDHD and was diagnosed later in life. She runs this course as an educator and peer, not as a therapist or counsellor.

Common questions

Do I need a formal diagnosis?

No. Everyone is welcome, whether you have a formal diagnosis, are self-identified, or are still working it out.

Where are the live sessions held?

Online. You will be emailed the link before the first session. Cameras, mics and sharing are always optional, and you are welcome to take part by chat only.

What if I miss a live session?

That's okay. The lessons, exercises and reflection prompts are there whenever you need them.

What if the course brings up a lot for me?

Take breaks whenever you need to. Crisis and support lines are one click away on every page, through the "Need support now?" button.
