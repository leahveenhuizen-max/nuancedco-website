What makes this course different

  • Made for the grief. Most post-diagnosis support covers strategies. This course makes room for the loss, anger and relief that come first.
  • Built for AuDHD. For autistic, ADHD and AuDHD adults together, not one diagnosis or the other.
  • Live, on Australian time. A Friday evening live group with Leah at 7pm AEDT, alongside short lessons you do at your own pace.
  • Come as you are. Cameras optional, chat-only welcome, no deadlines on the lessons.

Relief and grief can both be true.

Your five weeks

Hover over a week to see what it covers.

  1. 01

    Week 1

    Naming it

    Relief and grief can arrive at the same time, often tangled up with shame and doubt. This module gives those feelings names, so they stop feeling like proof that something is wrong with you.

  2. 02

    Week 2

    Recognise the losses

    Naming specific losses makes grief easier to hold than a vague heaviness. This module helps you put words to what you are grieving.

  3. 03

    Week 3

    Acknowledge the anger and the what ifs

    Anger is a normal part of grief. This module gives you permission to feel it, without letting it run the show.

  4. 04

    Week 4

    Understand who you are now

    A diagnosis can make you question which parts of you are really you. This module helps you separate your brain from the mask and the shame, and start unmasking at a pace that feels safe.

  5. 05

    Week 5

    Move forward

    Grief doesn't finish on a schedule, and moving forward doesn't mean leaving it behind. This module is about carrying it more gently and building a life that fits your brain.

Your guide

Hi, I'm Leah.

Registered Nurse, Mental Health First Aid facilitator and Master of Counselling student. I'm AuDHD and was diagnosed later in life, so this course comes from lived experience as well as study. I run it as an educator and peer, not as a therapist or counsellor.

  • Who it's for: autistic, ADHD and AuDHD adults in the months or years after a late diagnosis. It's written through an AuDHD lens, with notes wherever experiences tend to differ. It's especially for anyone feeling relief and grief at the same time and unsure where to turn.
  • What it is: education, reflection and peer connection, guided by a Registered Nurse and Master of Counselling student who has been through it.
  • What it isn't: therapy, counselling, diagnosis or medical advice. Anyone who needs more support is pointed to their GP and the services in the resources section.

Common questions

Do I need a formal diagnosis?

No. Everyone is welcome, whether you have a formal diagnosis, are self-identified, or are still working it out.

Where are the live sessions held?

Online. You will be emailed the link before the first session. Cameras, mics and sharing are always optional, and you are welcome to take part by chat only.

What if I miss a live session?

That's okay. The lessons, exercises and reflection prompts are there whenever you need them.

What if the course brings up a lot for me?

Take breaks whenever you need to. Crisis and support lines are one click away on every page, through the "Need support now?" button.
