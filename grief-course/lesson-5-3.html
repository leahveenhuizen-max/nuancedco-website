  • A small ritual: write a letter to your younger self, plant something, or play a song that marks this chapter
  • One connection: reach out to one person who gets it, online or in real life, in the next month
  • Know your supports: save the crisis lines, and consider a neurodiversity-affirming GP or counsellor if grief feels stuck or overwhelming

When to get extra support: if grief is stopping you from functioning day to day for a long time, or you're having thoughts of not wanting to be here, please speak with your GP or call Lifeline on 13 11 14.
