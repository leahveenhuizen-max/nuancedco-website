  • Two sets of needs, often missed: support for one condition may not have helped the other
  • Contradictions that looked like failure: needing routine but craving novelty, or hyperfocusing then crashing, may have been read as inconsistency
  • Double masking: hiding both autistic and ADHD traits at once

If you're autistic: losses may cluster around social connection, sensory overload and burnout.

If you're ADHD: they may cluster around unfinished study, jobs, money and missed deadlines.

If you're both: your map may hold a bit of everything.
