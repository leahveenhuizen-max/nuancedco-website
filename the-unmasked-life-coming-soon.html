Inside the course

Four weeks of evidence-informed education and reflection

Week 01

It Finally Makes Sense

Understand your ADHD brain and the life review that can follow late diagnosis.

Week 02

Grief for What Could Have Been

Make space for sadness, anger and the what-ifs without drowning in self-blame.

Week 03

The Mask, the Shame and the Big Feelings

Notice masking, shame, emotional intensity and what starts to shift when you stop performing fine.

Week 04

Building a Life That Fits

Create ADHD-friendly supports, systems and self-trust around your actual brain.
