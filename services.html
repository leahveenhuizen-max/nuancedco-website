How we work together

Support that actually understands you.

Pain point

Being told to 'try harder'

You already know your brain works differently, but the advice you keep receiving is generic, unhelpful, and exhausting.

Solution

Brain-based consultancy

We work with your real wiring, not a template. That means strategies built around your attention, energy, and sensory rhythm.

Pain point

Feeling unheard in healthcare

Services often minimise your experience or reduce it to a checklist, leaving you with more questions than answers.

Solution

Lived-experience grounded support

As an AuDHD founder and registered nurse, Leah blends professional understanding with real neurodivergent insight.

Pain point

Training that feels irrelevant

Most development programs are built for neurotypical brains, so they land flat for people who process information differently.

Solution

Tailored learning pathways

Courses, resources and sessions are structured to make sense for neurodivergent learners, with clear practical steps and sensory-aware pacing.

Pain point

Workplace safety is only surface-level

Organisations often talk about inclusion without changing the systems that create stress and burnout for neurodivergent staff.

Solution

Systems-level workplace consulting

We help teams build practical frameworks for psychological safety, communication, and sustainable workload design.
