About the creator

Meet Leah

Leah Veenhuizen is the founder of Nuanced Co. and creator of The Unmasked Life™ methodology. She's a neurodivergent consultant, executive function coach, and author working with late-diagnosed ADHD and autism adults in Geelong, Australia.

After her own late diagnosis at 24, Leah spent a decade helping adults understand the gap between their capability and their capacity. What started as 1:1 coaching evolved into a comprehensive framework designed specifically for the neurodivergent brain.

Today, she works with individuals and organizations to build sustainable structures that actually fit how neurodivergent people's brains work.
