## Script

---

Welcome to The Unmasked Life.

I am Leah. I am a registered nurse, mental health educator and neurodivergent consultant, and I built this course because I could not find what I was looking for. So eventually I made it.

This course is for late-diagnosed ADHD adults who want to understand their brain, their past and their support needs without being told to fix themselves.

That is a very specific brief, and it is deliberate.

---

Before we get into the content, I want to tell you a few things about how this works and what to expect.

**First: there is no rush.**

There are no automatic deadlines on this content. You are not behind. If you need to sit with something for longer than a week, you are allowed to do that. If something lands heavily and you need to step away before continuing, that is not a failure. That is an appropriate response to real material.

**Second: this is education, not therapy.**

I want to be really clear about that, not because it limits what is available here, but because I think it is important that you know what you are working with.

This course will give you frameworks, language, evidence and reflection tools. It will not replace one-on-one mental health support. Some of what comes up as you go through this content may feel big: relief, grief, anger, recognition, or all of those at once. That is normal. If it moves into territory that feels like it needs more than a course can hold, the support page has resources for finding qualified, neurodivergent-affirming practitioners.

**Third: you do not have to feel any particular way about your diagnosis.**

Some people come to a course like this feeling grateful for their diagnosis. Some come feeling furious. Some feel nothing yet. Some feel all of it in the same afternoon. There is no correct emotional response to being diagnosed late, and nothing in this course requires you to arrive at a positive framing you do not actually feel.

---

Here is how the course is structured.

There are four weeks.

Week 1 is The Late Diagnosis Disruption. We start with what late diagnosis actually does: to your understanding of your past, to the labels you were given, to the story you have been telling about yourself.

Week 2 is Masking, Overcompensation and Self-Blame. We go into the patterns that many late-diagnosed adults have been using to get through: appearing fine, over-functioning, perfectionism, people-pleasing, and what those have cost.

Week 3 is Support Needs, Executive Function and Environment. We get practical about how ADHD actually affects daily functioning, and what real support looks like.

Week 4 is Rebuilding Self-Trust After Diagnosis. We finish with what it means to move forward with a better and more accurate understanding of yourself.

---

Each week has a video lesson. You can also read the content without the video, or use the speed-read version if you need to move quickly. Workbook prompts are built into each week — you do not have to complete them all, but the ones that feel relevant are worth sitting with.

There is also a milestone at the end of each week. A simple marker. Tick it when it feels true, not when you think you are supposed to have arrived.

---

One more thing.

This course does not promise transformation. It promises accurate information and a space to process it without shame.

That may sound modest. I do not think it is. I think understanding yourself clearly is one of the most useful things available to you right now, and I think you probably came here because some part of you already knows that.

Start whenever you are ready. Week 1 is below.
