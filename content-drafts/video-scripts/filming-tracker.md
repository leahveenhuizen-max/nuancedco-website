## Metricool Scheduling Notes

- Connect Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and any additional channels in Metricool before scheduling.
- Use Metricool's Best Time to Post feature once accounts have 2 to 4 weeks of audience data.
- Batch scheduling: prepare 2 to 4 pieces of content at a time before scheduling.
- Suggested initial posting cadence: 3 to 4 posts per week while in waitlist phase.
- Use Metricool analytics to identify which formats and topics perform best within the first month, then weight future content toward those.
- TikTok and Instagram Reels are the primary short-form channels. Facebook and Instagram feed posts can carry longer-form educational content.

## Outfit Guidance

All Nuanced Co. videos use the palette: cream, plum, sage, muted rose, chocolate, soft black.
Avoid: tight patterns, noisy jewellery, rustling fabric, logos.
Maintain consistent lighting, background, framing and audio for all course lesson videos.
