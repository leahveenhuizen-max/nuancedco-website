## Script

Late ADHD diagnosis is not just information. It is a disruption.

When you find out you have ADHD as an adult, something starts happening that nobody really prepares you for.

You start looking back.

At the job you lost. At the relationships that fell apart. At every time you were called lazy, or disorganised, or too sensitive, or too much.

And you start seeing those things differently.

Not because ADHD explains everything. But because there was a better explanation than the one you were given — and no one gave it to you.

That is the disruption.

The relief and the grief and the anger and the "so that is what that was" all arriving at the same time, in a life that was already built around the wrong information.

If you are in that place — recently diagnosed, or diagnosed a while ago and still making sense of it — that is exactly what The Unmasked Life course is designed for.

It is four weeks of research-informed education for late-diagnosed ADHD adults who want to understand their brain, their past and their support needs.

No self-improvement framing. No productivity hacks. Just accurate information and space to process it.
