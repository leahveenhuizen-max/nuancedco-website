## Script

---

I was diagnosed with ADHD as an adult.

And the thing that surprised me most was not the diagnosis. It was everything that came after it. The looking back. The "so that is what that was" moments. The grief. The relief. The long list of things I needed to re-examine.

I spent a lot of time looking for resources that understood that experience. Resources that did not start from the assumption that ADHD was something to fix, or that I was behind, or that I needed a productivity system.

I mostly could not find what I was looking for.

So eventually I made it.

---

My name is Leah Veenhuizen. I am a registered nurse, a mental health educator and a neurodivergent consultant. I am also AuDHD (autistic and ADHD), and I was diagnosed as an adult.

I built Nuanced Co. because I wanted a space that took late-diagnosed ADHD adults seriously. Not as a problem to be optimised. As people who needed accurate information, delivered without shame, from someone who understood the experience from the inside.

---

Everything at Nuanced Co. is research-informed. That matters to me because I know the ADHD space is full of content that sounds compelling but is not grounded in anything solid. I want what I make to be genuinely useful, which means it needs to be accurate.

What Nuanced Co. offers is education. Not coaching. Not therapy. Not clinical treatment. Structured, evidence-informed education about ADHD, late diagnosis, masking, executive function, burnout and what support actually looks like. From someone who knows both the research and the experience.

---

If you have been diagnosed recently and you do not know what to do with that information, you are in the right place.

If you have been diagnosed for a while but still feel like you are not quite making sense of it, you are also in the right place.

You can explore the course, access support resources and service directories, or book a one-on-one consultation. Everything is on the website.

I am glad you are here.
