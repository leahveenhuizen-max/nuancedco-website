## Script

This is what Nuanced Co. is.

It is a neurodivergent consultancy. It creates research-informed ADHD education for late-diagnosed adults.

It is not a coaching programme. It is not a therapy service. It is education: accurate, evidence-informed, built by someone who is late-diagnosed herself.

If you have been diagnosed with ADHD as an adult and you are trying to understand what that actually means: for your past, for how your brain works, for what support looks like, this is built for that.

What we offer:

One-on-one consultancy with Leah, for people who want individual support within scope.

The Unmasked Life: a four-week research-informed course on late diagnosis, masking, executive function and rebuilding self-trust.

PDF guides and resources for people who want to work independently.

And support directories and service navigation for people who need help finding the right professional support.

Everything is at nuancedco.com.au.
