## Script

If you were diagnosed with ADHD as an adult, recently or not so recently, you probably already know that most ADHD content was not made with you in mind.

Most of it is aimed at kids. Or at hyperactive teenagers. Or at productivity optimisation.

What it is not often aimed at is the person who spent thirty, forty, fifty years not knowing, and is now trying to make sense of a life that looks a little different with this information in it.

That is what The Unmasked Life is for.

It is a four-week course for late-diagnosed ADHD adults who want to understand their brain, their past and their support needs. Without being told to fix themselves. Without a self-improvement framing. Without shame.

The course covers late diagnosis and the life review, masking and its costs, executive function and real support, and rebuilding self-trust after diagnosis.

It is research-informed. It is built by someone who is also late-diagnosed. And it takes your experience seriously.

The course is not open for enrolment yet. But the waitlist is.

Join the waitlist at the link below and I will let you know when doors open, including early access for founding members.
