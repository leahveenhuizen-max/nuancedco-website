# PDF Layout Quality Review — Nuanced Co.
Internal document. Ratings: ready to sell / sellable with minor edits / needs redesign / needs rewrite and redesign.

## 1. The Late Diagnosis Life Review (live Stan product, $19)
**Rating: cannot be rated — file not in project.**
Assessed from the cover JPG only: the cover is warm and on-palette (cream, purple, sage shapes) but typography is not the site's Cormorant Garamond display style, the layout is soft-collage rather than editorial, and it reads closer to a polished Canva cover than the site's premium editorial look. If the interior matches the cover style, expect "sellable with minor edits" at best and "needs redesign" to reach premium. Leah must supply the live file (see inventory) for a real verdict.

## 2. ADHD Burnout Guide (free, 4 pp)
**Rating: sellable with minor edits as a FREE resource; needs redesign if converted to paid.**
- Cover: clean, purple side rail, clear hierarchy, scope note on cover. Professional handout standard.
- Typography: Helvetica throughout. Off-brand (site is Cormorant + Atkinson Hyperlegible). No serif display anywhere.
- Readability/margins/spacing: good; three-column signs table is dense on mobile screens but acceptable.
- Page rhythm: text-heavy, one table, numbered step blocks; no diagrams.
- Premium feel: no. Competent professional handout, not a paid product. Does not damage trust as a freebie.
- Brand match: partial (palette yes, type no).

## 3. Getting an ADHD Diagnosis in Australia (free, 4 pp)
**Rating: sellable with minor edits as a FREE resource.**
Same template and verdict as the Burnout guide. Strongest of the three in usefulness (tables of who-can-diagnose and costs). Step labels and tables are the most visually structured content in the set.

## 4. AuDHD Executive Dysfunction Guide (free, 4 pp)
**Rating: sellable with minor edits as a FREE resource.**
Same template. Definition blocks give reasonable rhythm. No diagrams. Same off-brand typography.

## 5. Course companion PDFs (pdf-exports/, 5 files, internal)
**Rating: Welcome + Week 1 — sellable with minor edits (layout). Weeks 2–4 — needs rewrite (content too thin), layout fine.**
- On-brand: Cormorant Garamond display covers, Atkinson body, ink full-bleed covers, cream interior, branded footer with page numbers, embedded fonts, A4.
- Week 1 (11 pp) has real page rhythm: lesson sections, parchment workbook prompts, dark quick-reference spread, notes. This is the closest thing in the project to the premium standard the paid suite should use.
- Weeks 2–4 are 4 pages each because the manuscript is ~300 words per week; they would feel thin as paid standalone products.
- These are course assets; course is on hold, so none should be sold yet.

## Summary
- The layout system in `pdf-tools/` (Week 1 output) is the premium template to standardise on for all paid PDFs, including the Life Review redesign.
- The three free guides are fine as free credibility builders but are visibly a different (older, Helvetica) generation from the brand.
- Nothing currently in the project is "ready to sell" at a $19–$29 premium tier except, potentially, a redesigned Life Review built on the pdf-tools pipeline.
