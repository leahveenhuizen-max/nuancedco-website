Relief, Grief and Anger
There is no correct response
There is no correct emotional response to a late ADHD diagnosis. Some people feel relief — a framework that finally fits. Some feel grief. Some feel anger. Some feel nothing yet.
Relief and grief are not opposites. They can co-exist, alternate, and return.

Relief and grief at the same time
"Relief and grief are not opposites. Both can be true at the same time. There is no requirement to resolve one before you are allowed to feel the other."
This combination is one of the most commonly reported responses to late ADHD diagnosis in adults. You are not the first person to hold both of these at once.
