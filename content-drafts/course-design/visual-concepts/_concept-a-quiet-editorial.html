Scene 2 — Something Changed
What a late diagnosis actually does
A late ADHD diagnosis is not simply receiving new information. It is information arriving inside a life that was already built around explanations that were wrong, incomplete, or missing entirely.
When that information arrives, it does not stay in the present. It travels backwards. Across your life. Through your past. Into every place where something did not make sense.
"The disruption that follows — to identity, to self-understanding, to the story you tell about yourself — is a recognised part of what late diagnosis involves."

You chose: lazy
What people may have seen
Tasks left unfinished. Starting things but not completing them. Difficulty getting going even on things that mattered.
What may have been happening underneath
Task initiation — getting started — is a specific executive function that works differently in ADHD brains. What looked like not wanting to do something may have been a neurological difficulty starting it.
Support that may have helped
External structure, a starting prompt, or understanding that the difficulty was real — not a choice.
