# Nuanced Co. Gold Standard Definition

What "gold standard" means for this brand, stated as testable rules. Each standard has a pass test that anyone (Leah, a contractor, an AI session) can apply before shipping. If a page or asset fails its test, it does not ship.

---

## 1. Creative standard

Nuanced Co. work is calm, literary and adult. It looks and reads like a beautifully made book about a hard subject, not like a wellness funnel, a clinic, or a Canva workbook.

**Pass test:** put the asset next to a School of Life spread, an NDI workbook page and a generic Canva ADHD freebie. If a stranger groups it with the first two, pass. One ownable visual device (the map/constellation world) appears on every asset.

## 2. Learning standard

Every learning unit follows: arrive safely → one idea → make it personal → keep something. No unit delivers more than one core concept per screen. Every week ends with an artefact in the learner's own words.

**Pass test:** each scene/screen can be summarised in one sentence; each week produces something the learner can print, copy or save; a learner returning after two weeks away can resume in under ten seconds without shame cues.

## 3. Evidence standard

"Research-informed" is shown, never just claimed. Course teaching content carries a visible references section (APA 7), cited sources are peer-reviewed and within ten years unless historically necessary, and each week states a "content reviewed [month year]" line. No statistic ships without a source Leah can produce.

**Pass test:** for any factual claim in course or sales copy, the source can be located within one minute. The public Evidence page exists and is linked from the course sales page.

## 4. Accessibility standard

WCAG 2.2 AA as the floor, ADHD-specific design as the point: three formats per lesson (video, audio, speed-read), low-capacity route with genuinely shortened content (not the same text minus scenes), reduce-motion honoured everywhere, Atkinson Hyperlegible body text, autosave on every input, no time pressure anywhere.

**Pass test:** keyboard-only walkthrough completes every interaction; contrast checker passes; a lesson can be completed meaningfully in under ten minutes via the low-capacity route; nothing punishes absence.

## 5. Commercial standard

Every product names its price, audience and next rung on its own page. One primary CTA per screen. No urgency mechanics, no inflated discounts, no "free" applied to substantive education. Paid content sits behind a boundary (gate, or at minimum unlinked and noindexed during beta).

**Pass test:** a stranger can list the full product ladder with prices after five minutes on the site; no page contains two competing primary CTAs; no paid asset is reachable from a search engine.

## 6. Emotional safety standard

Content anticipates grief, shame and low capacity. Support boundaries appear at the point of need, not only in footers. Australian crisis pathways are one click from any heavy content. Scope statements ("education, not therapy") appear on every product page and every heavy course scene, in warm language, never defensive boilerplate.

**Pass test:** from the heaviest scene in any week, a distressed learner reaches 000/Lifeline information in one click; no copy anywhere moralises about effort, discipline or consistency.

## 7. Mobile standard

Designed for a tired adult on a phone in bed. Every page usable at 390px with no horizontal scroll, no overlapping floating elements, tap targets 44px+, course scenes readable in one thumb-scroll rhythm.

**Pass test:** full course walkthrough on a real phone with no pinching, no collisions, no wrapped headers; Snapshot copy/print works on iOS Safari.

## 8. Course standard

The Week 1 candidate architecture (scene journey, route choice, snapshot artefact, autosave) is the template for all four weeks. Weekly continuity is real: each week opens by reflecting something the learner wrote the week before. Leah is present in every week through recorded audio or video, not placeholders. Dashboard state is always true (real progress, no demo data, no dead players).

**Pass test:** all four weeks match the locked sequence in both title and body; no placeholder text is visible anywhere a learner can reach; every "note from Leah" plays actual audio.

## 9. PDF standard

Paid PDFs are keepsake-grade artefacts: cover art from the brand's visual world, typeset (not slide-deck), fillable where useful, screen and print friendly, with interior previews shown before purchase. Each carries scope note, references and version date.

**Pass test:** shown two spreads, a buyer cannot mistake it for a Canva template; the product page shows at least three interior pages before the buy button.

## 10. Copy standard

Australian spelling. No em dashes. No emojis in professional copy. Warm, confident, plain: no justifying language, no hype, no clinical distance. Leah is a neurodivergent consultant, registered nurse and mental health educator, never coach/therapist/psychologist/counsellor/clinician. Sentences short enough to hold with taxed working memory.

**Pass test:** automated scan finds zero em dashes, zero "word,word" collision typos, zero banned role words, zero "free" on substantive education; read-aloud test: every paragraph survives being read to a tired friend.

## 11. Founder-presence standard

Leah is visible, credentialed and human at every commercial and emotional key moment: homepage (name, face, one-sentence credibility), course sales page (photo, not placeholder), each course week (voice), each PDF (a short signed note). Credibility is always one sentence: lived experience plus registration plus educator role together.

**Pass test:** count the screens between any entry point and the first appearance of Leah's face or voice: must be 2 or fewer on the website, 1 or fewer in the course.

## 12. Visual-design standard

One design system across site and course: shared palette (cream/cocoa/plum/ochre), shared type (Cormorant Garamond display, Atkinson Hyperlegible text), shared motif (map and constellation), light theme public, dark theme course, deliberately. No page ships on a legacy template. Imagery is crafted (illustrated map world, real photography of Leah), never stock, never emoji, never clip-art.

**Pass test:** any two screens from anywhere in the ecosystem, side by side, read as the same brand; the fossil-page count is zero.

## 13. Trust standard

Everything the brand says about itself is currently true: no fake progress states, no "coming soon" older than one release cycle, no dead links, no placeholder dates, working forms with working success pages, accurate legal pages, consistent ABN and scope statements.

**Pass test:** monthly link-and-form crawl returns zero 404s and zero placeholder strings ("to be confirmed", "coming soon", "[", "PLACEHOLDER") on public or learner-reachable pages.

---

These thirteen standards are the review gate for every future session: benchmark first, then build, then test against this document before anything ships.
