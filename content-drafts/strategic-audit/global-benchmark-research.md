# Global Benchmark Research

Prepared for Nuanced Co. strategic audit. Researched July 2026 using current web sources. These are reference points, not templates. Nothing here should be copied; the purpose is to understand what the best operators in adjacent spaces do well, and where Nuanced Co. can genuinely differentiate.

Scoring: relevance to Nuanced Co.'s specific position (research-informed ADHD education for late-diagnosed adults, founder-led, Australian, education-only scope, paid PDF plus course ladder).

---

## 1. Neurodivergent Insights (Dr Megan Anna Neff)

- **URL:** https://neurodivergentinsights.com
- **Market:** US, global reach
- **Audience:** Late-identified autistic, ADHD and AuDHD adults, plus clinicians
- **Offer model:** Free articles and infographics build audience. Paid digital workbooks (roughly USD 10 to 40), themed bundles, a clinician library, a Substack membership, and trade-published books (The Autistic Burnout Workbook). Clear ladder from free insight to paid depth.
- **Visual style:** Soft, muted palettes, hand-finished infographics, Venn diagrams that became a signature format. Instantly recognisable and screenshot-friendly.
- **UX strengths:** Store is organised by problem (burnout, sensory, executive functioning), not by format. Every product page shows interior spreads so buyers can judge quality before paying.
- **Copy strengths:** Writes from inside the experience while citing research. First person, precise, never hyped. Uses "late-identified" framing that mirrors the reader's own language.
- **Trust signals:** Clinical credential (Psy.D.) stated plainly, lived experience (AuDHD) stated plainly, publications with major publishers, citations in articles.
- **Accessibility:** Strong visual chunking; some long article pages get heavy. PDFs are designed for printing and screen.
- **Conversion approach:** Content-first. Free article answers the question, then offers the workbook that goes deeper. No urgency mechanics at all.
- **What Nuanced Co. can learn:** This is the closest successful analogue to Leah's model (credentialed founder, lived experience, paid PDFs, education-only scope). Note how the credential and the lived experience are always presented together, in one sentence. Note interior previews on every paid PDF.
- **What to avoid copying:** The Venn diagram visual signature is hers. Also avoid the sheer volume strategy; Neff publishes constantly, which is not viable for a solo founder alongside a nursing career.
- **Relevance: 10/10**

## 2. Inflow (getinflow.io)

- **URL:** https://www.getinflow.io
- **Market:** UK/US, global app
- **Audience:** Adults with ADHD, diagnosed or exploring
- **Offer model:** Subscription app. CBT-informed psychoeducation modules, daily exercises, community. Published RCT evidence of symptom improvement. Acquired by Cerebral in 2026, which signals commercial validation of ADHD psychoeducation as a category.
- **Visual style:** Warm gradients, rounded cards, generous whitespace, one idea per screen.
- **UX strengths:** The defining pattern: micro-dosed learning. Modules are broken into screens of one to three sentences, with a single interaction per screen. Progress is visible, sessions are completable in minutes, and re-entry after absence is shame-free.
- **Copy strengths:** Second person, present tense, zero moralising. Anticipates drop-off and writes for the returning user.
- **Trust signals:** "Science-backed" claims are backed by an actual published RCT and named clinical advisors, not vague "evidence-based" labels.
- **Accessibility:** Mobile-first by definition; short text blocks suit working memory constraints.
- **Conversion approach:** Free trial into subscription; quiz onboarding personalises the pitch.
- **What Nuanced Co. can learn:** One idea per screen is the single most transferable pattern for the course. The Week 1 candidate's scene structure is directionally right; the amount of text per scene is still web-article density, not Inflow density.
- **What to avoid copying:** Subscription pressure mechanics and gamified streaks. Also, Inflow's scale of production (a full app) is not the bar; the pacing principle is.
- **Relevance: 9/10**

## 3. Future ADHD (Grace Koelma)

- **URL:** https://futureadhd.com
- **Market:** Australia, global sales
- **Audience:** ADHD adults, heavily Instagram-acquired
- **Offer model:** Premium digital planner PDF (100,000+ copies sold in two years), annual editions, a Penguin-published book (The ADHD Focus Friend), podcast. Proof that Australians will pay meaningful money for a well-designed ADHD PDF.
- **Visual style:** Soft, contemporary, decidedly not clinical. The planner itself is the brand asset; spreads are shown constantly.
- **UX strengths:** Product pages sell with large previews, video walkthroughs and concrete page counts. Nothing is abstract.
- **Copy strengths:** "Built with input from 100,000+ neurodivergent people", "works with your brain, not against it". Specific numbers, shame-free framing.
- **Trust signals:** Sales figures, publisher deal, community scale. Less clinical authority, more social proof.
- **Accessibility:** Planner is customisable to different brains; site itself is standard Shopify-ish.
- **Conversion approach:** Instagram content demonstrates the product daily; the site closes the sale with previews and testimonials.
- **What Nuanced Co. can learn:** The paid PDF category has a proven ceiling far above AUD 19 when the artefact looks premium and the previews prove it. The Late Diagnosis Life Review and Companion Guides need visible interior spreads to sell.
- **What to avoid copying:** The productivity-planner positioning. Nuanced Co. is education and reflection, not planning. Also avoid the volume of Instagram dependency; Leah's channel mix is different.
- **Relevance: 9/10**

## 4. How to ADHD (Jessica McCabe)

- **URL:** https://howtoadhd.com
- **Market:** US, global
- **Audience:** ADHD adults and their supporters ("Brains" and "Hearts")
- **Offer model:** YouTube channel (1M+ subscribers) funding via sponsorship and membership; NYT-bestselling book with a deliberately ADHD-friendly layout.
- **Visual style:** Friendly, bright, hand-drawn toolbox motifs.
- **UX strengths:** The book's layout is the benchmark: chapter summaries at the start, key points highlighted, explicit "if you only read one thing" paths. Format options respect variable capacity.
- **Copy strengths:** Radical warmth without infantilising. Names community members as co-creators.
- **Trust signals:** Longevity (since 2015), expert interviews woven into content, transparency about her own failures.
- **Accessibility:** Multiple consumption formats; captions; chaptered videos.
- **What Nuanced Co. can learn:** The multi-path reading pattern (full journey, low capacity, speed read) that the Week 1 candidate already has is validated here; the execution needs to be as visible and celebrated as McCabe's "read this chapter first" signposting.
- **What to avoid copying:** The chatty YouTube voice. Leah's register is calmer and more adult, which is a differentiator, not a gap.
- **Relevance: 8/10**

## 5. The Mini ADHD Coach (Alice Gendron)

- **URL:** https://www.theminiadhdcoach.com
- **Market:** France, global English-language audience
- **Audience:** Late-diagnosed ADHD adults, especially women
- **Offer model:** Free illustrated content built a half-million follower audience; monetised through a trade-published visual guide and digital products.
- **Visual style:** A single recognisable illustrated character and consistent doodle style. The visual identity IS the brand.
- **UX strengths:** Every concept is one image. Complexity is translated into a relatable moment.
- **Copy strengths:** Tiny amounts of text doing large emotional work. "Diagnosed at 29" framing mirrors the audience.
- **Trust signals:** Lived experience and audience scale rather than credentials.
- **What Nuanced Co. can learn:** One distinctive visual device, used everywhere, is worth more than ten generic design flourishes. Nuanced Co.'s Old Map / New Map metaphor could be that device if it is drawn, not just written.
- **What to avoid copying:** The illustration style itself, and the credential-light positioning. Leah's clinical credibility is a structural advantage Gendron does not have; it should be far more visible than it currently is.
- **Relevance: 8/10**

## 6. ADDitude Magazine

- **URL:** https://www.additudemag.com
- **Market:** US, global
- **Audience:** ADHD adults, parents, clinicians
- **Offer model:** Free editorial at enormous scale, expert webinars (free live, paid replay ecosystem), paid magazine, directories.
- **Visual style:** Dated magazine-portal aesthetic. Functional, cluttered, heavy ad load.
- **UX strengths:** Topic taxonomy is excellent; a reader with a specific question lands on a specific answer.
- **Copy strengths:** Every piece is expert-reviewed and bylined, with credentials attached.
- **Trust signals:** The masthead model: named medical review panel, expert bylines, decades of archive.
- **Accessibility concerns:** Ad density and popups actively hostile to attention-impaired readers. A cautionary tale.
- **What Nuanced Co. can learn:** Expert review and bylines convert to trust. Even as a solo founder, "reviewed against current literature, [date]" notes on course content would borrow this power honestly.
- **What to avoid copying:** The ad clutter and the volume treadmill.
- **Relevance: 6/10**

## 7. Unconventional Organisation (Skye Rapson)

- **URL:** https://www.unconventionalorganisation.com
- **Market:** New Zealand and Australia, serves globally
- **Audience:** ADHD adults and workplaces
- **Offer model:** Research-backed ADHD coaching, workshops, courses and workplace programmes. Doctoral-candidate founder.
- **Visual style:** Clean, professional, somewhat corporate.
- **UX strengths:** Clear service segmentation for individuals versus organisations.
- **Copy strengths:** "Strengths-based, research-backed" positioning carried consistently; blog articles cite studies.
- **Trust signals:** Founder's academic standing, ADDA directory presence, media appearances.
- **What Nuanced Co. can learn:** How an Australasian founder-led brand handles the individual/workplace split navigation without confusing either audience. Also how "research-backed" is substantiated by actually citing research in content.
- **What to avoid copying:** Coaching language. Nuanced Co. must never drift toward "coach" framing.
- **Relevance: 8/10**

## 8. Embrace Autism

- **URL:** https://embrace-autism.com
- **Market:** Canada, global
- **Audience:** Autistic and AuDHD adults, especially late-identified and high-masking
- **Offer model:** 300+ free articles and self-tests build enormous trust; paid formal assessments are the revenue engine.
- **Visual style:** Plain, almost academic. Proof that trust can outweigh visual polish.
- **UX strengths:** Self-tests give the visitor immediate personal value and a reason to return.
- **Copy strengths:** Every claim referenced. Footnotes everywhere. The citations are the brand.
- **Trust signals:** Clinician founder with lived experience; verifiable credentials; explicit neurodiversity-affirming stance.
- **Accessibility:** Text-dense pages; fine for readers, heavy for skimmers.
- **What Nuanced Co. can learn:** Referencing is a moat. Nuanced Co. already plans an Evidence Base and References document; it should be visible, public and prominent rather than buried in the vault.
- **What to avoid copying:** The visual plainness, and the assessment offering (out of scope for Leah).
- **Relevance: 8/10**

## 9. The School of Life

- **URL:** https://www.theschooloflife.com
- **Market:** UK, global
- **Audience:** Adults seeking emotional education
- **Offer model:** The canonical emotional-education product ladder: YouTube (6M+ subscribers), books, card sets, stationery, classes, workshops, corporate programmes. Every product reinforces one coherent voice.
- **Visual style:** Distinctive typographic system, muted sophisticated palette, consistent art direction across every artefact. The gold standard for "premium calm".
- **UX strengths:** Shop and content feel like one continuous world. Product photography treats a card deck with the seriousness of jewellery.
- **Copy strengths:** "Calm, reassuring, and sane" house voice. Speaks to painful subjects (anxiety, regret, self-knowledge) with dignity and zero clinical coldness.
- **Trust signals:** Institutional tone, publishing pedigree, physical schools.
- **What Nuanced Co. can learn:** This is the emotional register Nuanced Co. is reaching for: adult, literary, warm, unhurried. Also the lesson that a small number of beautifully produced artefacts beats a large number of adequate ones.
- **What to avoid copying:** The institutional facelessness. Leah's founder presence is an asset The School of Life deliberately suppresses; Nuanced Co. should do the opposite.
- **Relevance: 9/10**

## 10. Ness Labs (Anne-Laure Le Cunff)

- **URL:** https://nesslabs.com
- **Market:** UK, global
- **Audience:** Knowledge workers interested in evidence-based thinking about minds and work
- **Offer model:** Free weekly newsletter built the audience; paid membership with community and self-paced courses; trade book (Tiny Experiments). Founder is a neuroscience researcher, and that identity anchors everything.
- **Visual style:** Minimal, editorial, generous type. Feels like a calm publication, not a funnel.
- **UX strengths:** Newsletter-to-membership path is frictionless and unhyped.
- **Copy strengths:** Research translated into plain language with citations linked inline. "Mindful productivity" framing avoids hustle culture entirely.
- **Trust signals:** Active academic affiliation, consistent citation practice.
- **What Nuanced Co. can learn:** How a single credentialed founder converts research literacy into a premium brand without ever sounding clinical or salesy. The closest tonal benchmark for Leah's writing voice.
- **What to avoid copying:** The productivity audience framing.
- **Relevance: 8/10**

## 11. The Gottman Institute

- **URL:** https://www.gottman.com
- **Market:** US, global
- **Audience:** Couples, and a large professional/clinician tier
- **Offer model:** Textbook two-sided ladder: consumer products (books, card decks, workshops like The Art and Science of Love) and professional certification (Levels 1 to 3, 135,000+ clinicians trained). Each side reinforces the other's credibility.
- **Visual style:** Warm, professional, research-forward.
- **UX strengths:** Audience split (couples versus professionals) is the first navigation decision, and everything downstream stays in lane.
- **Copy strengths:** Evidence is quantified and specific: "50 years of research with 3,000 couples", "94% report positive results". Never vague.
- **Trust signals:** Named researchers, published studies, outcome statistics.
- **What Nuanced Co. can learn:** The way evidence is expressed: specific numbers, named sources, plain sentences. Also the long-term possibility of a practitioner-facing tier (facilitator guide already exists in planning), which raises consumer trust even before it launches.
- **What to avoid copying:** The certification empire scale and the slightly corporate polish.
- **Relevance: 7/10**

## 12. Finch

- **URL:** https://finchcare.com
- **Market:** US, global app
- **Audience:** Young adults and neurodivergent users doing self-care
- **Offer model:** Freemium self-care app with a virtual pet; heavily adopted by ADHD users.
- **Visual style:** Soft, rounded, gentle.
- **UX strengths:** The masterclass in shame-free re-entry: no streak punishment, no guilt messaging, micro-sized asks ("drink a glass of water"). CBT techniques present but never named as homework.
- **Copy strengths:** Asks about pronouns and emotional needs up front; treats the user as fragile-day-capable.
- **Trust signals:** Community word of mouth; 229,000+ donated subscriptions signal values.
- **What Nuanced Co. can learn:** The emotional mechanics of returning after absence. The course's "Hit a wall?" box is the right instinct; Finch shows how deep that principle can go (autosave everywhere, celebrate any re-entry, never show a decayed progress state).
- **What to avoid copying:** Gamification and the pet metaphor; wrong register for grieving adults.
- **Relevance: 7/10**

## 13. Yellow Ladybugs

- **URL:** https://www.yellowladybugs.com.au
- **Market:** Australia
- **Audience:** Autistic girls, women and gender-diverse people, their families and professionals
- **Offer model:** Charity: events, professional training, books, resources. Neurodivergent-led.
- **Visual style:** Distinctive warm illustration-led identity that reads as neither childish nor clinical.
- **UX strengths:** Clear pathways for three audiences (individuals, families, professionals).
- **Copy strengths:** Identity-affirming language that Australian ND communities recognise as genuinely insider.
- **Trust signals:** ND-led governance, government partnerships, national reputation. Already linked from Nuanced Co.'s support page.
- **What Nuanced Co. can learn:** Australian ND-community credibility norms: lived-experience leadership stated plainly, co-design acknowledged, community consultation visible.
- **What to avoid copying:** Charity positioning and event-centric model.
- **Relevance: 6/10**

## 14. Mindvalley (cautionary benchmark)

- **URL:** https://www.mindvalley.com
- **Market:** Global
- **Audience:** Mass-market personal development
- **Offer model:** Subscription access to high-production self-paced "quests" with daily micro-lessons.
- **Visual style:** Cinematic, high-gloss, celebrity-teacher-led.
- **UX strengths:** The "quest" pacing model (one short lesson per day, visible cohort) genuinely drives completion. Portal UX is best-in-class for making self-paced content feel alive.
- **Copy strengths (and weakness):** Extremely persuasive, but routinely overpromising; transformation claims without evidence.
- **Trust concerns:** Hype mechanics (countdowns, inflated discounts) that sophisticated audiences distrust and that would be fatal for a scope-bounded health-adjacent brand.
- **What Nuanced Co. can learn:** Portal craft only: daily-sized units, a visible "today" state, beautiful media players, momentum cues.
- **What to avoid copying:** Every persuasion mechanic. This is the anti-benchmark for tone.
- **Relevance: 5/10**
