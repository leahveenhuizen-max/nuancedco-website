# Free Resource Audit — Nuanced Co.
## Internal document. Not for publication.

Version: Draft 1.0
Last updated: June 2026

This audit reviews items currently described as free across the Nuanced Co. site. It does not implement any changes. All decisions require Leah's approval before action is taken.

---

## Policy context

Free public resources at Nuanced Co. may be limited to:
- Australian crisis and mental-health support directories
- Urgent-help information
- Public service navigation
- GP preparation information
- Support-service lists
- Brief general signposting resources

Substantive ADHD educational material (guides, workbooks, in-depth explainers) is not free. Nuanced Co. is not giving away course content or paid educational guides.

## Items audited

### 1. ADHD Burnout Guide

**Current location/wording:** Listed in downloads.html as a free download resource.

**Nature of content:** Substantive ADHD educational material. An in-depth guide explaining ADHD burnout, its causes, patterns, and recovery considerations.

**Is this public signposting or substantive education?** Substantive education.

**Recommendation:** Convert to paid, or replace with a brief signposting version (one page, linking to support resources and the paid guide). The full guide should not be offered free if the product model is to sell educational resources.

**Likely direction:** Convert to paid product or shorten to a free signpost. Leah to decide.

**Links/copy affected:** downloads.html listing, any footer or nav references to free resources, any social posts promoting this guide as free.

### 3. ADHD Diagnosis Australia Guide

**Current location/wording:** Listed in downloads.html as a free download resource.

**Nature of content:** Navigation and service information. Content likely covers how to get an ADHD diagnosis in Australia: where to go, what to expect, GP referral pathways, assessment processes.

**Is this public signposting or substantive education?** Primarily public service navigation, which may fit the free-resource policy.

**Recommendation:** Review content. If the guide is primarily a service directory and process explainer (not a clinical or therapeutic resource), it may remain free. If it contains significant educational content about ADHD itself, shorten the free version and make the educational portions paid.

### 4. Footer wording: "free resources on neurodivergence"

**Current location/wording:** The footer email subscription form includes the line: "Sign up to receive updates, free resources on neurodivergence, and news from Nuanced Co."

**Nature of content:** Footer copy making a general promise of free resources.

**Is this public signposting or substantive education?** Ambiguous. "Free resources on neurodivergence" is broad enough to imply substantive educational material.

**Recommendation:** Flag for Leah's decision. Options:
- Change to "Sign up to receive updates, support resources and news from Nuanced Co." (removes the "free" framing)
- Change to "Sign up to receive updates, service navigation resources and news from Nuanced Co." (makes the type of free resource explicit)
- Leave as-is if the email list will genuinely send free educational content (but this should then be reconciled with the free-resource policy)

**Links/copy affected:** Footer of all pages that use the shared footer template (this appears to be a site-wide footer component). Change would affect all pages.

**Do not change without Leah approval.**
