# The Unmasked Life — Reader Edition Plan
## Internal planning document. Not for publication.

Version: Draft 1.0
Last updated: June 2026

---

## Purpose

The Reader Edition is a complete, standalone version of The Unmasked Life course content in PDF format. It is designed for people who prefer to read and work independently, without video lessons or a live group format.

It is a paid product in its own right.

It is also included as a course material for participants in the full beta and ongoing course.

It is not a free download, a teaser or a sample. It contains the complete educational content of the four-week course.

---

## Target Reader

- Late-diagnosed ADHD adults who do not need or want video content
- People who prefer to read at their own pace
- People who want to work through the material privately before or instead of joining the group format
- People who have completed the full course and want a portable reference
- People who cannot attend a facilitated beta cohort at the scheduled time

## How Worksheets Should Be Handled

The Reader Edition integrates reflection prompts inline rather than as separate downloadable worksheets. Each teaching section ends with one or two clear reflection questions formatted as a distinct visual element (shaded box or rule separator).

A separate printable worksheet for each chapter can be offered as an add-on or included in the full course Resource Vault, but the Reader Edition is complete without them.

Design note: The Reader Edition should be designed so that blank space is provided after each reflection prompt, allowing the reader to write directly into the PDF if using a tablet or to print and write by hand.

---

## How References Should Be Handled

References are integrated as in-text notes (author, year) in the teaching body where a specific finding or framework is named.

A full reference list appears in Appendix B.

All references must comply with the Nuanced Co. Evidence and Referencing Standard v1.1.

The teaching voice is educational, not academic. References are present to give readers confidence in the evidence base without disrupting the reading experience.

## How to Avoid Cannibalising the Full Course

The Reader Edition and the full course serve different learners and different preferences.

Key differentiators to preserve:

| Reader Edition | Full Course |
|---|---|
| Self-paced, fully independent | Facilitated, community-supported |
| Read and reflect alone | Video, platform and live group |
| Available any time | Cohort-based (beta) or evergreen (future) |
| Single purchase | Higher-priced, more supported |
| No live interaction | Weekly catch-ups during beta |

Marketing language should make these distinctions clear. The Reader Edition is not a "budget course." It is a different format for a different learner.

The Reader Edition should be priced meaningfully lower than the full course, but not so low that it positions the course as overpriced. The suggested price range is approximately $69 to $79 AUD for later review by Leah. Final pricing to be confirmed before launch.

The Reader Edition must not cannibalise the full course. It serves a different learner: self-paced, independent, no live interaction needed. Marketing language must make the distinctions clear. The Reader Edition is not a "budget course." It is a different format for a different learner.

The founding beta includes the Reader Edition. Companion Guides remain separate paid products and may be offered as checkout add-ons or in a bundle at a small saving.

## How Week 1 Translates into the Reader Edition Chapter

Chapter 1: The Late Diagnosis Disruption

The Week 1 course content in week-1.html has been fully rebuilt and provides the basis for this chapter. The Reader Edition chapter follows the same structure and teaching, adapted for standalone reading rather than platform navigation.

### Chapter structure

**Chapter opening (one paragraph)**
Brief statement of what this chapter is for: understanding what a late ADHD diagnosis does to your understanding of your past, yourself, and the story you have been telling about who you are.

**Section 1: What late diagnosis actually does**
Adapted from the Learn 01 accordion on week-1.html. Teaching about information arriving late inside a life already built around incorrect explanations. Written in teaching voice, not bullet points.

**Section 2: Was that ADHD too?**
Adapted from Learn 02. The retroactive review stage: why it happens, why it is not self-pity, what to do with the recognition when it arrives. Includes one inline reflection prompt.

**Section 3: Identity disruption**
Adapted from Learn 03. The disruption to the story built around old labels. The temporary instability of the sense of self when the old explanations no longer hold. Includes one inline reflection prompt.

**Section 4: The emotional landscape**
Adapted from Learn 04. Relief, grief, anger, numbness. No correct response. Old labels and their weight. Includes one inline reflection prompt.

**Section 5: Executive function: your brain's management system**
Adapted from Learn 05. Executive function as the brain's management system. Examples. Note that Week 3 (Chapter 3) covers this in depth. Includes one inline reflection prompt.

**Section 6: Explanation versus excuse**
Adapted from Learn 06. The distinction between accurate explanation and excuse. Why this matters. No reflection prompt needed here.

**Section 7: Grief and relief (the Reframe section)**
Adapted from the Reframe card on week-1.html. Grief as a reasonable response to real loss. Grief versus self-pity. Support that was missing then and what may help now. Two inline reflection prompts.

**Section 8: The Life Review Map**
Adapted from the Apply section. The five-step structured exercise. Inline prompts formatted with blank space for handwriting or tablet annotation. Note at end distinguishing this from the Late Diagnosis Life Review standalone PDF.

**Quick reference: That Was ADHD Too?**
One-page summary at the end of the chapter. Can be printed and used separately. Styled distinctly from the teaching body.

**Companion Guide mention**
At the close of the chapter: "If the grief side of this chapter is landing heavily, the Companion Guide for this chapter is Grieving the Life You Thought You Had. It is available separately for $29 AUD and goes deeper on the grief, what it asks of you, and how to carry it without collapsing into self-blame."
