## Reader Edition Content

The Reader Edition is a standalone PDF containing the core teaching from all four weeks.

### What belongs in the Reader Edition:
- All four week introductions
- Core teaching sections for each week (the equivalent of the lesson content)
- Key concepts and frameworks, clearly explained
- Reflection prompts (can be the same as or adapted from the workbook prompts)
- Week milestones
- Quick reference summaries for each week
- Signposting to Companion Guides and further resources
- Evidence and referencing section or footnotes
- Scope disclaimer page
- About Leah and Nuanced Co. page

### What does NOT belong in the Reader Edition:
- Live group catch-up content
- Platform-specific features (milestone ticks, video embeds, accordion UI)
- Internal facilitator notes
- Beta feedback requests
- Links to the course portal (it is standalone by design)
