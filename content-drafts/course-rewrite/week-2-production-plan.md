# Week 2 Production Plan — Masking, Overcompensation and Self-Blame

Implementation-ready plan, written against the Week 1 candidate's scene structure (`week-1-production-candidate.html`) and the existing `week-2-rebuild-brief.md`. This plan does not build the page — it specifies what the build should contain.

Status: planning document. Do not begin rebuilding `week-2.html` until Leah has reviewed this plan in addition to the original brief.

---

## Proposed six-scene structure

Following the Week 1 candidate's pattern (one route-selection opening scene, four content scenes, one closing/Snapshot scene):

1. **Week Opening** — orientation, route selection (Full Journey / Low-Capacity / Speed-Read), resume block. Lesson video placeholder. "A note from Leah" audio placeholder.
2. **What the Mask Is** — defines masking without conflating it with deception; distinguishes conscious from unconscious masking; introduces AuDHD-specific masking patterns. Saved reflection: "Where do you recognise masking in your own life?"
3. **The Strategies** — perfectionism, people-pleasing, over-functioning as masking strategies, each explained with "what it looks like from outside" versus "what it costs underneath" (mirrors Week 1 Scene 3's reveal-panel pattern: select a strategy, reveal what's underneath, write your own interpretation feeding the Snapshot).
4. **The Cost** — cognitive load, exhaustion, identity confusion, delayed recognition. Uses a checklist/selection pattern similar to Week 1 Scene 4 (EF areas) but for masking costs (energy, relationships, identity, work, health). Selecting costs feeds the Snapshot's "what masking has cost me" field.
5. **Self-Blame and the Wrong Explanation** — the difference between self-awareness and self-blame; what happens when the wrong explanation gets internalised. Emotion-chip pattern similar to Week 1 Scene 5, but tuned to masking-specific feelings (shame, disorientation, relief, grief, anger, confusion about identity) with the same kind of validating response logic.
6. **Build Your Map** — the Unmasking Map worksheet (see below) plus the Week 2 Snapshot assembly, milestone, weekly catch-up panel, and Week 3 preview.

This keeps full structural parity with Week 1: same six-scene shape, same Snapshot pattern, same closing elements, different content underneath.

## Week 2 learner output: "My Masking and Self-Blame Map"

Same pattern as the Week 1 Snapshot — five fields, auto-populated from scene interactions where possible, editable, copyable, printable, visible throughout via the same lightweight indicator pattern (relabelled "My Map" or kept as "My Snapshot" for consistency — recommend keeping the indicator label generic, e.g. "My Week 2 Map," so the UI pattern reads as consistent across weeks).

Proposed fields:
1. **The mask I recognise most** — auto-fed from Scene 3 strategy selection
2. **What it has cost me** — auto-fed from Scene 4 cost selection
3. **What I used to call this** (the self-blame label) — auto-fed from Scene 5
4. **What I understand differently now** — free text, Scene 5 or 6
5. **One thing I am noticing, not yet changing** — free text in Scene 6 (deliberately framed as noticing, not committing to unmask, per the brief's safety note)

- AuDHD-specific masking patterns
- Perfectionism reframed specifically as a *coping pattern*, not only a personality trait

- Week 1's specific recognition lines, label set (lazy/inconsistent/careless/etc.), and EF area list — these are Week 1-specific content and must not reappear verbatim; Week 2 needs its own label set (the self-blame labels people internalised from masking, e.g. "fake," "too much," "attention-seeking," "performing") and its own cost categories.
