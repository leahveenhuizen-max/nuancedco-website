Booking enquiry

Book a call

For adults, workplaces or organisations wanting educational support around ADHD, late diagnosis, neurodivergent support needs, mental health literacy or practical next steps.

This is an enquiry pathway, not instant clinical support. Leah will be in touch within two business days.

Ways we can work together

1:1 Educational Consultation

A real conversation about your brain, your experience, and what practical next steps might look like for you. No scripts, no checklists. You don't need to arrive with answers.

Nuanced Integration Session

Designed for people who have recently received a diagnosis or are making sense of existing information. Focused, reflective and at your pace.

Workplace or Professional Enquiry

For organisations and teams wanting to improve understanding of neurodivergent support needs, psychological safety or inclusive practice.

What to expect

Your first consultation is a real conversation. Leah listens to what is actually happening for you, not what a checklist says should be happening.

You do not need to arrive with answers, a diagnosis, or a plan. You need to arrive as yourself.

"You do not need to become someone else. You need to understand the person you already are."

About Leah
